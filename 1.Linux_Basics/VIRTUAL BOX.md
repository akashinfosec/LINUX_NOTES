VirtualBox is software that creates a virtual computer inside your existing computer, allowing you to run another operating system like Linux without changing your main operating system.

===VirtualBox is software that lets you create a "computer inside your computer.

## Simple Analogy 🏠

Imagine your laptop is a **house**.

Inside that house, you build a **small room**.

The room has:

- Its own bed
- Its own table
- Its own electricity
- Its own rules

Even though it's inside the same house, it functions independently.

A **VirtualBox virtual machine (VM)** is like that room.


## How Does It Work?

Your physical laptop shares some of its resources with the virtual machine.

For example:

Your laptop has:

- 💻 16 GB RAM
- 🧠 8 CPU cores
- 💾 512 GB SSD

When creating a VM, you might assign:

- 4 GB RAM
- 2 CPU cores
- 30 GB storage

The VM uses only those resources while it's running.


##### **===VirtualBox vs EC2**


|VirtualBox|EC2|
|---|---|
|Runs on your own laptop|Runs in Amazon's data center|
|Works even without internet (after setup)|Requires internet access|
|Uses your laptop's hardware|Uses Amazon's hardware|
|Free to use|Pay based on usage|
|Great for learning|Great for real-world deployment|
